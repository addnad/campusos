## What you post

You keep ownership of your notes, messages and files. By sharing them in
a course room you allow other students in that room to see them, and you
allow us to store and display them for that purpose.

Do not post:

- Anything that harasses, threatens or bullies another student
- Sexual content
- Someone else's personal information — including photographs of
  documents belonging to other people
- Material you have no right to share
- Advertising, referral links or anything you are being paid to promote
- Deliberately false academic information, such as an invented deadline

Course rooms are shared spaces for coursemates. Treat them as you would a
lecture hall.

## Moderation

Students can report messages. Reports are reviewed by a person.

If a report is upheld, we may add a strike against you in that room. Two
strikes times you out of that room for a day. Three removes you from it
permanently. Removals in three separate rooms mean your account is
reviewed.

We may remove content or suspend an account without warning where there
is a risk to someone.
