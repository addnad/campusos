# ADR-004: Communities Belong to Curriculum Courses

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-30  
**Decision Owner:** CampusOS Team

---

## Context

CampusOS needs collaboration spaces where students can interact.

Possible community structures include:

- University communities.
- Programme communities.
- Department communities.
- Course communities.

The most meaningful collaboration happens around shared academic responsibilities.

---

## Decision

CampusOS communities will be connected primarily to Curriculum Courses.

A student joins communities based on the courses they are enrolled in.

---

## Reasoning

Students naturally collaborate around:

- Assignments.
- Lecture materials.
- Exams.
- Difficult topics.
- Course discussions.

A course-based community provides immediate relevance.

---

## Consequences

Positive:

- Higher quality discussions.
- Easier peer discovery.
- Strong academic context.

Negative:

- Less general university-wide social interaction initially.

---

## Future Consideration

Broader communities such as programme, university, or interest groups may be introduced later.
