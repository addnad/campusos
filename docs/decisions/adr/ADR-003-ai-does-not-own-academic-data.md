# ADR-003: AI Does Not Own Academic Data

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-30  
**Decision Owner:** CampusOS Team

---

## Context

AI will play an important role in CampusOS through recommendations, tutoring, summaries, and academic assistance.

However, allowing AI systems to become the source of academic truth creates reliability and trust problems.

---

## Decision

AI will not own academic data.

Academic information such as courses, assignments, curriculum structures, and student records will be owned by their respective product domains.

AI will only consume existing data and generate assistance.

---

## Reasoning

CampusOS should maintain a clear separation:

Academic systems provide context.

AI provides intelligence.

This ensures students can trust that academic information comes from structured sources rather than generated responses.

---

## Consequences

Positive:

- Higher trust.
- Easier data validation.
- Better system architecture.

Negative:

- AI features require well-structured underlying data.

---

## Future Consideration

AI capabilities can expand as more academic context becomes available.
