## Vision

We envision a future where every university student has a single academic home that understands their journey, eliminates academic chaos, and helps them reach their full potential through organization, intelligence, and collaboration.

## Mission

CampusOS exists to become the home of a student's academic life by bringing organization, learning, and collaboration into one context-aware platform.

## North Star

Every student should know exactly what they need to do next to succeed.

## Why CampusOS Exists

University life is fragmented.

Students constantly switch between messaging apps, note-taking apps, cloud storage, emails, learning portals, calendars, screenshots, and conversations with classmates just to stay organized.

CampusOS exists to unify this fragmented experience into a single trusted academic home that understands a student's academic context and proactively helps them succeed.

Instead of forcing students to manage university life manually, CampusOS organizes information, connects related academic activities, and provides intelligent assistance based on each student's unique academic journey.
